## How this log is used

- A decision moves to **Agreed** only when DX confirms it in writing or in a sprint review.
- Changing an agreed decision later is a change request, estimated and approved before work starts.
- Technical decisions with lasting architectural impact also get an ADR in `docs/adr/`.
